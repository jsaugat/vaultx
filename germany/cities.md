Loan context matters most: prioritize cities where your first year is financially stable.

**The cost reality of those three cities**

| City      | Avg. Student Rent (cold) | Monthly Living Cost estimate |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Munich    | €800–1,100/room          | €1,400–1,800                 |
| Berlin    | €600–900/room            | €1,100–1,500                 |
| Stuttgart | €600–800/room            | €1,000–1,400                 |

With a blocked account around **€11,208/year** (about **€934/month**), Munich is usually too tight without extra funding.

Better value cities with solid CS options and Werkstudent potential: **Dortmund, Dresden, Leipzig, Karlsruhe, Kaiserslautern** (Hamburg as a mid option).

**Recommendation:**

- First choice: affordable strong-CS cities (especially Karlsruhe, Dresden, Dortmund, Kaiserslautern)
- Second tier: Berlin
- Avoid Munich on a full loan unless you have scholarship/support
