### Blocker 4: Loan and Finances — What You Need to Know Before You Borrow

German student visa requires proof of funds: currently **€11,208/year** in a blocked account (Sperrkonto) — about €934/month. This is a visa requirement, not optional.

This money must sit in a German blocked account before your visa is approved. You draw it monthly once in Germany.

On a full loan this means your loan must cover:
- Sperrkonto amount (~€11,000+)
- Tuition (FH: €1,500–€5,000/semester for international students, some are free)
- Flight + initial setup costs (~€1,500–€2,000)
- First 1–2 months before Werkstudent job starts

**Total loan needed before you even land: NPR 18–22 lakhs minimum** depending on the program. Make sure your loan covers this. Many Nepali students underborrow and hit a wall at the visa stage.
