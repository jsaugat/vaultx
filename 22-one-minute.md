## 22 one-minute habits to save 25+ hours/week

1. **Ask “What is today’s adventure?”**: Identify the most important task and frame it as an adventure to boost productivity and creativity.
2. **Put your most important task on the calendar**: If it’s not scheduled, it won’t get done—block time for key priorities.
3. **Start a focus timer**: Use a visible timer to stay on task and prevent procrastination.
4. **Put your phone face down and turn off notifications**: Minimize distractions by keeping your phone out of sight (ideally in another room).
5. **Keep “Do Not Disturb” on**: Reduce interruptions and “attention residue” so you can stay focused.
6. **Block distracting apps after 9 PM**: Prevent late-night scrolling that disrupts sleep and hurts next-day productivity.
7. **Keep your phone away from your bed**: Charge it across the room (or in another room) to avoid late-night phone use and improve sleep.
8. **Learn keyboard shortcuts for what you do most**: Save time in tools like Excel, Notion, Google Docs, or Slack by using the keyboard instead of the mouse.
9. **Use your keyboard to open apps**: Use Spotlight/Windows key (or tools like Alfred) to open apps faster.
10. **Set up text expansion**: Use TextExpander (or iPhone text replacement) for frequently typed phrases like emails, addresses, or VAT numbers.
11. **Use dictation**: Speaking is often faster than typing—use dictation tools to draft ideas or write.
12. **Practice the “capture” habit**: When you think of something you need to do, write it down immediately in a task system to free mental space.
13. **Organize contacts with WhatsApp lists**: Categorize friends/family/work (e.g., with emojis) to quickly spot important messages and reduce anxiety.
14. **Listen at faster speeds**: Train yourself to listen to audiobooks/podcasts at 1.5x, 2x, or even 3x.
15. **Double-dip (multimodality multitasking)**: Combine different task types (e.g., audiobook while commuting) to maximize time efficiency.
16. **Do walking Zoom calls**: Take meetings while walking to get steps/sunlight, boost creativity, and avoid separate exercise time.
17. **Use a treadmill standing desk**: Add walking to your workday (meetings, light tasks, even gaming) to rack up steps effortlessly.
18. **Use recurring calendar blocks**: Schedule regular commitments (date nights, gym, etc.) to reduce coordination overhead.
19. **Batch organizing recurring tasks**: When booking something (e.g., date night), book several in advance to reduce context switching.
20. **Schedule important activities at 8 AM**: Early commitments encourage earlier sleep and can improve next-day productivity.
21. **Hire a cleaner**: Outsource chores you dislike (especially if it costs less than your hourly rate) to free up time.
22. **Hire an executive assistant (for business owners)**: Delegate email/calendar/ad-hoc tasks to focus on higher-value work.
