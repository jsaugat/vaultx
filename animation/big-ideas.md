# Six Fundamental Principles of Animation for Beginners

These core principles help create animation that feels more natural, expressive, and believable.

## The 6 Big Ideas of Animation

### 1. Speed

The distance between objects across frames determines how fast they appear to move.

- **Objects spaced farther apart** → appear faster
- **Objects spaced closer together** → appear slower

Spacing directly controls perceived motion speed.

---

### 2. Acceleration & Deceleration (Easing)

Natural motion rarely starts and stops abruptly.

To make movement feel realistic:

- Start slowly
- Speed up through the middle
- Slow down before stopping

This creates smoother and more believable transitions.

---

### 3. Squash & Stretch

Changing an object's shape during movement adds energy and realism.

Examples:

- **Stretching** during fast motion emphasizes speed
- **Squashing** during impact or direction changes suggests force

This principle also communicates material properties:

- More squash/stretch → softer, flexible object
- Less squash/stretch → rigid, hard object

---

### 4. Anticipation

Small preparatory movements before a major action.

Purpose:

- Builds tension or energy
- Signals upcoming movement
- Helps the audience understand what is about to happen

Example: A character bending their knees before jumping.

---

### 5. Overshoot

A follow-through effect where an object moves slightly past its final position before settling back.

This adds:

- Weight
- Springiness
- More natural stopping motion

Without overshoot, motion can feel mechanical or abrupt.

---

### 6. Arcs

Most natural movement follows curved paths rather than straight lines.

Using arcs creates:

- Smoother motion
- Organic flow
- More visually pleasing animation

Straight-line movement often feels robotic unless intentionally used.

---

# The Golden Rule

## Ask Yourself:

> **"How does this motion feel?"**

If movement feels:

- Stiff
- Unnatural
- Mechanical
- Unconvincing

Use these principles to diagnose and refine the animation until it feels right.
