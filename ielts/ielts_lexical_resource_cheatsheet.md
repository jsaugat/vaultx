# IELTS Lexical Resource Cheat Sheet

## 1. Accuracy

| Weak / Incorrect | Better | Why |
|---|---|---|
| freedomness | autonomy | Correct academic word |
| people gets benefit | people benefit | Correct structure |
| more easier | easier | Avoid double comparative |
| highly advantage | highly beneficial | Correct collocation |
| do a progress | make progress | Natural verb pairing |
| knowledgeful | knowledgeable | Correct form |

### More examples

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| solvement | solution |
| discuss about | discuss |
| effect to people | affect people |

---

## 2. Naturalness (Formal Academic Tone)

| Weak / Awkward | Better | Why |
|---|---|---|
| keep their brain calm | reduce mental strain | Academic phrasing |
| make people more smart | enhance intellectual development | Formal precision |
| too much pressure comes | excessive pressure arises | Natural formal structure |
| work gets done fastly | tasks are completed efficiently | Proper adverb / phrasing |

### More examples

| Casual / Spoken | IELTS-Friendly |
|---|---|
| a lot of problems | numerous challenges |
| kids learn stuff | children acquire knowledge |
| things get bad | conditions deteriorate |
| people feel good | individuals experience improved well-being |

---

## 3. Variety

### Repetition Problem

| Repetitive | Varied Version |
|---|---|
| important, important, important | significant, crucial, essential |
| good | beneficial, positive, advantageous |
| bad | harmful, detrimental, adverse |
| big | substantial, considerable, significant |

### More substitutions

| Basic | Alternatives |
|---|---|
| help | assist, facilitate, support |
| show | demonstrate, illustrate, indicate |
| improve | enhance, strengthen, optimize |
| problem | issue, challenge, obstacle |

**Rule:** Do not force random synonyms.

Bad:
> children, offspring, juveniles, minors

Better:
> children, young people

---

## 4. Precision

| Vague | Precise |
|---|---|
| bad effects | detrimental consequences |
| good results | measurable benefits |
| money problems | financial constraints |
| stress issues | psychological strain |
| work problems | operational inefficiencies |

### More examples

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| technology is growing | technological advancement is accelerating |
| education helps | education enhances employability |
| pollution is bad | air pollution poses serious health risks |

---

## 5. Less Common but Natural Vocabulary

| Overdone / Forced | Better |
|---|---|
| ubiquitous ramifications | widespread consequences |
| profoundly multifaceted phenomenon | complex issue |
| indispensable quintessential factor | essential factor |

### Good Academic Vocabulary

| Theme | Useful Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| Government | public expenditure, state intervention, fiscal policy |
| Education | curriculum reform, academic achievement, skill acquisition |
| Technology | automation, digital infrastructure, innovation |
| Work | productivity, flexibility, professional development |
| Environment | sustainability, carbon emissions, conservation |

---

## 6. Collocations (Very Important)

| Wrong / Awkward | Correct |
|---|---|
| do a decision | make a decision |
| heavy advantage | significant advantage |
| strong pollution | severe pollution |
| big knowledge | extensive knowledge |
| high crime | high crime rate |

---

## 7. Cause–Effect Chains

### Weak
Remote work is good because people are happy and productive.

### Better
Remote work reduces commuting-related stress, which improves concentration and enhances productivity.

---

## 8. Upgrade Patterns

### Pattern 1
**Basic:** X is good  
**Better:** X offers considerable benefits

### Pattern 2
**Basic:** X causes problems  
**Better:** X gives rise to significant challenges

### Pattern 3
**Basic:** X helps people  
**Better:** X enables individuals to

### Pattern 4
**Basic:** X is becoming common  
**Better:** X has become increasingly prevalent

---

## Quick Self-Check Before Submitting

Ask:

- Is this accurate?
- Does this sound natural?
- Am I repeating words?
- Can I be more precise?
- Am I forcing fancy vocabulary?

If yes to the last question, simplify it.
