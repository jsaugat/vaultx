# IELTS Writing Task 1 Vocabulary Bank

---

# Describing Visuals

- illustrates
- depicts
- presents
- shows
- outlines
- demonstrates
- provides information about
- presents a breakdown of
- compares
- highlights
- summarizes
- gives an overview of

Examples:

- The chart **illustrates** changes in employment rates.
- The table **presents a breakdown of** household spending.

---

# Referring to Categories

- in terms of
- with regard to
- regarding
- concerning
- as for
- when it comes to

Examples:

- **In terms of** population growth...
- **With regard to** energy consumption...

---

# Ranking / Position

- emerge as the most ...
- rank first
- lead the chart
- dominate
- occupy the highest position
- come second
- be followed by
- trail behind
- record the lowest figure
- rank last

Examples:

- Canada **emerged as the most popular destination**.
- Germany was **followed by** France.

---

# Percentages / Proportions

- accounting for
- representing
- constituting
- making up
- comprising
- standing at
- amounting to
- equivalent to

Examples:

- Housing costs **accounted for 24%**.
- The figure **stood at 45%**.

- only

- marginal

Examples:

- A **mere 10%**

## Large

- a substantial
- a considerable
- a significant
- a dominant
- the vast majority of

Examples:

- around
- about

- close to
- nearly
- almost
- just under
- just over

Examples:

# Comparison

- in contrast
- by contrast
- compared to
- as opposed to
- whereas
- while
- conversely
- on the other hand

Examples:

- **In contrast**, sales declined.
- Rural areas, **as opposed to** cities...

# Describing Similarity

- similarly
- likewise
- in the same way

- mirrored

Examples:

# Describing Differences

- outnumber
- exceed
- surpass
- outpace
- lag behind
- fall short of

Examples:

- Cars **outnumbered** motorcycles.
- Imports **surpassed** exports.

## Decrease

- plateau

## Small Change

# Patterns

- an inverse pattern
- an upward trend
- a downward trend
- a fluctuating pattern
- a consistent trend
- a noticeable shift

Examples:

# Making Observations

- it is evident that

- it is clear that
- notably
- interestingly
- overall

Examples:

- **It is evident that** urban growth was faster.

# Useful High-Level Phrases

- shows a steady progression
