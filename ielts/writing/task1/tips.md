# IELTS Writing Task 1 Cheat Sheet

---

# Tips

- When comparing data in body paragraphs, always support statements with **specific numbers or figures**.  
  Example:  
  _The number of visitors increased significantly, rising from **25% to 40%**._

---

# Data Vocabulary

## For Higher Values

- over
- just over
- above
- more than

**Examples:**

- The figure was **just over 50%**
- Sales were **above 200 units**

---

## For Lower Values

- below
- just under
- less than
- almost

**Examples:**

- The percentage was **just under 30%**
- The number was **less than 100**

---

## For Approximation

- about
- around
- approximately
- more or less

**Examples:**

- There were **around 500 people**
- The value was **approximately 75%**

---

# Comparison Vocabulary

- compared to
- as opposed to
- whereas
- while
- in contrast to

**Examples:**

- Car ownership was higher in urban areas **compared to** rural areas.
- The northern region saw growth, **whereas** the south declined.

---

# Map Vocabulary

## Location Description

- is located
- is situated
- lies
- is positioned

**Examples:**

- The school **is situated** near the town center.
- The park **lies** to the west of the river.

---

## Area Description

- residential area
- housing area
- commercial district
- industrial zone
- town center / city center

---

## Roads / Transport Features

- railway
- road
- main road
- highway
- pathway

### Movement / Position Verbs

- runs through
- crosses
- passes through
- goes through
- connects

**Examples:**

- A railway line **runs through** the eastern side.
- The main road **crosses** the river.

# IELTS Task 1 Layout

---

## 1. Introduction

Paraphrase the question.

**Example:**  
The chart illustrates the percentage of households owning different types of vehicles between 2000 and 2020.

---

## 2. Overview

Summarize the **main trends / key features only**.

Include:

- Highest point
- Lowest point
- Overall increases/decreases
- Major contrasts

**Example:**  
Overall, car ownership increased steadily, while bicycle ownership declined. Cars remained the most popular mode of transport throughout the period.

---

## 3. Body Paragraph A

Include:

- Detailed data
- Specific numbers
- Comparisons
- Early trends / first half of chart

**Example Structure:**

- Start with one category
- Add supporting figures
- Compare with another category

---

## 4. Body Paragraph B

Include:

- Remaining details
- Later trends / second half
- Additional comparisons

**Example Structure:**

- Describe remaining categories
- Highlight notable differences
- Support with exact figures

---

# Quick Writing Formula

### Introduction

Paraphrase

↓

### Overview

Big picture trends

↓

### Body Paragraph A

Detailed numbers + comparisons

↓

### Body Paragraph B

Remaining details + comparisons

---

# Golden Rule

**No opinions. No reasons. No explanations.**

Only:

- Describe
- Compare
- Report data accurately
