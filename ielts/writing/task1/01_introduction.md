## Paraphrase

- charts >> data
- map >> illustration
- shows / provides information >> presents, displays
- the data presents
  - the percentage of / proportion
  - the number of / the amount of
  - the change of / variation
  - the increase / growth in
  - the decrease / decline in
- subjects

## Examples

The table illustrates information about the total cost in billions of dollars for the years 1988, 1992 and 2000 while the pie charts display the data of how different countries share as the source of fish imported in the US.

## Tips

- first thing to do it find what doesnt go in the introduction
- **Currency names** are written in lowercase in normal sentences (e.g., 50 dollars, 10,000 rupees, pounds sterling), while only currency codes (USD, NPR, GBP) are capitalized and symbols ($, ₹, £) have no case rules.
- If two figures are given, remember to mention years for both of it, not just one
- "illustrates information about" → redundant
  Just say "illustrates the total cost..." — "information about" is filler.
- **"display the data of how different countries share as the source"** → unnatural phrasing
  "Share as the source" is awkward. Write it as: "show the proportion/sources of fish imported to the US by country"
- **"different countries"** → be specific or use the question's language
  The question says "sources of fish" — you can say "three countries (China, Canada, and others)" or simply "the sources of fish".
- maybe mention units at the end
