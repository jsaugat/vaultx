This is a **map/plan question** — slightly different from charts. Instead of describing trends, you're describing _changes over time_ between two maps. Let me write you a full model answer.

**Key observations before writing (always do this first):**

**Moved:** Marina (private yachts) moved south · Fishing boats moved north · Car park split into two

**Model answer (~200 words):**

> The two maps illustrate how Porth Harbour has changed between 2000 and the present day. Overall, the harbour has undergone significant development, with facilities expanding considerably in the southern area while the northern section has seen some reorganisation.

> In 2000, the northern part of the harbour contained showers and toilets, a marina for private yachts, and a car park along the western edge. The dock and passenger ferries were positioned to the east, with fishing boats occupying the central area. A lifeboat was stationed in the south, near a disused castle and a public beach.
>
> Today, the layout has changed considerably. The marina has been relocated to the southern section, and a new area of cafés and shops has been added alongside it. An additional car park and a second block of showers and toilets now serve this expanded southern zone. Meanwhile, the fishing boats have moved northward to occupy the space previously held by the marina. Notably, the disused castle has been removed from the current plan entirely.
>
> The public beach to the north and the passenger ferry terminal remain in their original positions, suggesting that the main changes have been concentrated in the southern and central parts of the harbour.

**Map-specific vocab to memorise for tomorrow:**

| Instead of | Use this                                                       |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| moved      | has been **relocated** · **shifted** · **transferred**         |
| added      | **introduced** · **constructed** · **established**             |
| removed    | **demolished** · no longer present · has **disappeared**       |
| next to    | **adjacent to** · to the north/south of · **alongside**        |
| changed    | undergone development · been **redeveloped** · **transformed** |

**The golden rule for map questions** — always use passive voice. "A car park _was added_" not "they added a car park." It sounds more academic and is naturally how maps are described.
