## Strong Opinion / Giving Views

- I firmly believe that…
- I strongly feel that…
- From my perspective…
- In my opinion…
- As far as I’m concerned… (Personal preference / softer)
- There’s no denying that… (Strong generally accepted point)
- I’d argue that… (Debatable argument with reasoning)
- Without a doubt…
- Absolutely
- To a large extent…

**Examples**

- I firmly believe that technology has transformed education.
- There’s no denying that cultural exchange broadens perspectives.

## Natural Intensifiers

Stop overusing “very.”

Use:

- incredibly
- remarkably
- exceptionally
- immensely
- highly
- particularly
- fairly
- relatively

**Bad:** very interesting
**Better:** genuinely fascinating / particularly interesting

# Your 2 stated goals

## 1. Improve grammatical accuracy

Focus on these structures:

**Conditionals**

- If I had the opportunity, I would…
- If people were more aware…

**Complex sentences**

- Although…, I believe…
- While some people think…, I would argue…

**Relative clauses**

- The place that I visited…
- The person who inspired me…

**Present perfect**

- I have always been interested in…

---

## 2. Expand lexical resource

Don’t memorize huge word lists blindly. That’s how people end up sounding robotic.

Do this:

Pick one topic (travel, education, technology, culture) and prepare:

- 10 strong adjectives
- 10 collocations
- 5 opinion phrases
- 5 examples

Example for **travel**:

- breathtaking scenery
- cultural immersion
- memorable journey
- broaden perspectives
- escape from routine

That’s far more effective than stuffing random fancy words into answers. IELTS examiners notice forced vocabulary immediately.
