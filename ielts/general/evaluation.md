# IELTS Evaluation Criteria (Quick Reference)

## 📘 Listening (4 sections, 40 questions)

- **Accuracy of answers** (correct spelling + grammar required)
- **Understanding main ideas**
- **Understanding specific details**
- **Following speaker opinion / attitude**
- **Handling accents and speed variations**

---

## 📖 Reading (40 questions)

- **Locating specific information (scanning)**
- **Understanding main idea (skimming)**
- **Inference (reading between lines)**
- **Vocabulary in context**
- **Matching headings / summaries accurately**
- **Speed + time management (critical factor)**

---

## ✍️ Writing (Task 1 + Task 2)

### Task Achievement / Task Response

- Addresses all parts of the question
- Clear purpose (describe, argue, compare, etc.)
- Relevant content (no off-topic writing)

### Coherence & Cohesion

- Logical structure (paragraphing)
- Clear progression of ideas
- Use of linking words (but not overused)

### Lexical Resource

- Range of vocabulary
- Accuracy of word choice
- Avoid repetition
- Correct spelling and word formation

### Grammar Range & Accuracy

- Sentence variety (simple + complex)
- Fewer grammatical errors
- Correct punctuation

---

## 🗣️ Speaking (3 parts)

- **Fluency & Coherence** (no long pauses, logical flow)
- **Lexical Resource** (vocabulary range + precision)
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy**
- **Pronunciation** (clarity, stress, intonation)
- **Natural communication** (not memorized answers)
